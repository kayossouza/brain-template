## Folders

- `Daily Notes/`: one note per day, created by `/daily`.
- `Inbox/`: captures from `/brain`, triaged by the `inbox-processor` agent or `/weekly`.
- `Projects/`: one folder per project; active ones have a `CLAUDE.md`.
- `People/`, `Companies/`, `Places/`, `Tools/`: one note each.
- `Decisions/`: decisions with a review date.
- `Finances/`: recurring bills and obligations with `next_due`.
- `Timeline/`: notes by year, month or week.
- `Areas/`: areas of life with a summary and linked notes.
- `Archives/`: finished or inactive content.
